The Basics Explained

Expand each card to go deeper. Slide myth ↔ fact to test your intuition.

AI Literacy

AI systems learn patterns from huge amounts of data. They don't 'know' facts — they predict what's most likely to come next based on examples.

Large language models are trained to guess the next word. Understanding this framing helps you spot what AI is good at and what it struggles with.

Responsible Use

Being responsible with AI means being honest about when you use it, careful with private data, and critical of what it tells you.

AI in Education

AI is changing homework, grading, and how we learn. That's not automatically good or bad — it depends on how we use it.

ARTICLES

Perspectives on AI, from students.

Real views on topics we've all been wondering about.

Ethics 6 min

When is using ChatGPT actually cheating?

A student's guide to drawing the line between tool and shortcut — with real classroom scenarios.

There's no single rule. What matters is intent, transparency, and the learning objective. If your teacher wants to see your reasoning, AI-generated answers rob you of practice. If the task is drafting or brainstorming, using AI as a partner can be perfectly legitimate — as long as you disclose it and still do the thinking.

Study 4 min

5 ways I actually study with AI (that aren't cheating)

From flashcard generation to Socratic-style tutoring, here's what works — and what backfires.

1. Turn your notes into practice questions. 2. Ask for a simpler analogy when you're stuck. 3. Have it quiz you and grade your explanations. 4. Get feedback on a rough draft before you polish. 5. Ask 'what am I missing?' after finishing a study session.

Future 8 min

The classroom in 2030

Personal tutors, adaptive assessments, and the teachers we'll still need.

The most exciting change isn't automation — it's personalization. Imagine every student getting an AI tutor that knows exactly where they're stuck. The teacher's job shifts from lecturing to mentoring, from grading to guiding.

Bias 5 min

Why AI mirrors the internet's blind spots

Training data determines behavior. Here's how to spot bias before it fools you.

AI models learn from what's online, and the internet isn't representative of every culture, dialect, or worldview. When you ask a model to describe 'a good student,' notice whose image comes up. Bias detection is now a literacy skill.
